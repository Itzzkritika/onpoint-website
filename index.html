Rewards Are Broken for Gen Z

Traditional loyalty programmes create friction, confusion and abstract value. Here's how we change that.

Points go in,
motivation goes out

  • ⌛

  • 🧩

📋 For The Judges
Our Strategic Framework

How Trip In Progress addresses Onpoint's four core challenges: Desirability, Clarity, Credibility, and Distinctiveness.

🔥
Desirability
Making Onpoint emotionally resonant for Gen Z by anchoring rewards to aspirational travel experiences, not abstract currency.
  • Trip-first interface design — destination before points
  • Progress-based dopamine loops (milestone celebrations)
  • Social sharing as a built-in feature, not an afterthought
  • Campaign aesthetics aligned with Gen Z's visual language
🔍
Clarity
Eliminating confusion around point value, earning rules, and redemption with radical transparency and plain-language UX.
  • 1 Onpoint = ₹1 — displayed prominently everywhere
  • Trip Meter translates points into days-to-goal, not abstract numbers
  • Partner eligibility displayed before purchase, not after
  • Spending breakdown shows exactly where points came from
🏆
Credibility
Building trust through real airline and hotel integrations, transparent conversion rates, and zero fee policy.
  • Named airline partners: Air India, IndiGo, Vietnam Airlines
  • Hotel brands: Taj, ITC — premium associations, premium trust
  • Real-time transaction confirmation and point allocation
  • No expiry, no minimum redemption thresholds
⚡
Distinctiveness
Carving a unique positioning as "the travel goal app" — not another cashback or points aggregator — through campaign and product.
  • "Trip In Progress" — no other platform owns this narrative space
  • Progress Card social mechanic creates organic differentiation
  • Double-dip positioning — uniquely stack with any existing card
  • Side Quest Fund campaign: distinctively Gen Z in tone and format
